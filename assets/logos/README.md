# Company logos

Drop each subsidiary's logo here and point its `logo` field at it in
`data/companies.js` (or upload it to the `logo` field in Sanity).

- **Format:** SVG is best; otherwise PNG or WebP with a transparent background.
- **Shape:** square-ish marks read best — the badge is a small square tile.
  A wide wordmark still works (it is fitted inside the tile) but gets small.
- **Size:** 256 × 256 px is plenty for PNG/WebP.
- **Colours:** the tile is near-white, so use the version drawn for a light
  background.

Until a company has a logo, its badge shows its initial on its brand colour.
